# FrontierSite Intelligence

### Geospatial intelligence for investment and infrastructure projects across Africa.

**From country-level risk to project-level decisions.**

FrontierSite Intelligence is an early-stage technology venture building AI-assisted, geospatial risk intelligence for organizations investing in, financing, and delivering projects across Africa.

We help project developers, investors, and development finance institutions understand **where projects may encounter governance and operational friction, what is driving those risks, and what to investigate before making consequential decisions.**

Our initial focus is **Kenya and East Africa**, particularly energy and infrastructure projects.

## What We're Building

- **SiteScan** — Location-specific due diligence that brings together governance, land, community, climate, and security considerations, with traceable sources and explicit uncertainty.

- **Monitor** — Ongoing monitoring of relevant policy developments, environmental hazards, and publicly reported events around project sites.

- **Governance Friction** — Analysis of local administrative capacity, regulatory implementation, land governance, and stakeholder dynamics that may affect project delivery.

- **Portfolio Intelligence** *(planned)* — A unified view of risk exposures across multiple project locations and assets.

The goal is to make complex, fragmented information easier to evaluate and act on throughout the project lifecycle: **screen, assess, monitor, and respond.**

## Our Approach

We are developing workflows that combine:

- **Geospatial analysis:** Site boundaries, infrastructure networks, satellite-derived signals, and environmental exposures.
- **Political and institutional analysis:** Local governance capacity, policy implementation, land arrangements, and political context.
- **Event and document intelligence:** Appropriately licensed event data, public records, research, and source-grounded AI-assisted synthesis.
- **Decision support:** Interpretable indicators, human review, audit trails, and clear communication of uncertainty.

Our intended engineering stack includes **Python, PostGIS, geospatial data pipelines, retrieval-augmented generation, and web-based analytical tools**. Architecture and implementation will evolve with product validation.

## What Matters to Us

**Evidence over black-box scores.** Risk assessments should be linked to verifiable sources, dates, geographies, and stated limitations.

**Responsible use of data.** We respect data licensing, privacy, confidentiality, and applicable research obligations.

**Human judgment in consequential decisions.** AI can accelerate analysis; material conclusions require appropriate validation and oversight.

**A clearly bounded use case.** Our work supports responsible project assessment and risk management. We do not build tools to identify or suppress political participants or vulnerable communities.
